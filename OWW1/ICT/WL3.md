flipped learning & self-regulated learning

flipping should:

- reduce cognitive overload
  (because you are more in control over pace etc.)

- enhance motivation: from self-determination theory.
(increase of autonomy bc more control; competence bc better prepared for tut session; relatedness bc less lecture time & more active learning -> more interaction)

self regulating =
- monitoring+adjust own learning (using cogntv + metacogntv strats)
- regulate motivation (to do the monitoring)

plan&goals -> stategies&monitoring -> reflect&adapt
(cyclse of self-reg learner)

important, because more autonomy in blended env
-> requires more self-regulation

srl is important life skill
(directly causes higher achievement)

srl is difficult and slow to develop by:
- doing tasks requiring it
- receiving specific explain/support/training

especially difficult for young people
(from adolescence more complex srl is possible)

home and school env impacts development of srl

how support/measure?

- goal setting
- time management
- environment structuring (aka where put smartphone etc.)
- help seeking (when stuck, do u know? and do you ask for help?)
- task definition (do you know what to do?)
- strategic planning (before) -> strategies etc.
- strategic planning (after) -> do you make changes?

in flipped environment?

- provide explicit srl instruction (increases srl quality)
  (ie. explain strategies)
- facilitate srl by prompting (increases quantity)
- regular reflection moments
- goal setting and planning
- progress monitoring (by students and/or teachers)

promts examples:
- which strats you gonna use?
- explain in own words the concept of ...
- reflect on approach. effective? changes?

research: srl prompts on their own did not impact performance
+ students did not see their meaning/relevance

adding explicit instruction -> increase in learning

conclusion:

- students need srl to fully benefit from flipped/blended env
- consider/support srl when designing
- blended learning can develop srl due to a need to apply
  (but takes time)

blended learning -> autonomy -> more oppurtunity to develop skills
