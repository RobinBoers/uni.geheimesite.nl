is blended learning effective?

key conditions:
- online and offline alignment
  (no disconnect, that reduecs understanding and engagement)
- active learning (activities, collaborative etc.)
- teacher presence & guidance; teacher as mentor, gives feedback etc. (also online, via ie. discussion boards)
- clear expectations and communications: what is expected; participations, deadlines, feedback, assesment.
- student motivation + regulation: more autonomy
- quality of tech & infra: reliable, user-friendly, available support etc.
- feedback & assesment intr. -> frequent/continuous, formative
^increase understanding & engagement
