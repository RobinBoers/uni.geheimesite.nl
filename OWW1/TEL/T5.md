intercultural comms = succesfull comms between people with different backgrounds

Byram's model:

- knowledge of social groups, products, practises, processes
- attitude of curiosity and openness + willing to revise values and beliefs

skills:
- discovering & interaction: acquire knowledge & operate on it in real-time conversations
- intepreting & relating: identify and explain perspectives & mediate between cultures

leads to => critical cultural awareness = look at things from the perspectives of different cultures

Deardorff's model:

1. knowledge: cultural self-awareness
2. internal outcome: frame-of-ref shift, aka *ability to change perspective*
3. external outcome: more effective and appropriate comms & behaviour in the intercultural situation
4. attitudes: respect, openness, curiosity, discovery
  (valuing other cultured, witholding judgement, tolerating ambiguity)

mediation = translation of perspective to the perspective of the other, keeping both in mind.

self-awareness of socialcultural and sociolinguistic behaviours

engage => create positive, neutral, trusted, shared "space"/environment

analyse "rich points": differences/difficulties (in comms) from constrasting viewpoints, comms styles, attitudes or behaviours.
