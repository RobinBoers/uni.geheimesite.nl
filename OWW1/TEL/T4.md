definitions:

- mediation = narrow gap between individuals/contexts/communities
- plurilingual competence = communicate with multiple languages
- linguaculture = interrelationship of language and culture

- communicative competence = act in foreign language
  - linguistically
  - sociolinguistically
  - pragmatically

- intercultural competence = interact with foreign culture
  - experience diversity
  - analyse and benefit from it
  - understand and mediate between cultures
  - question cultural environment

- pluricultural competence = participate in foreign culture
  - has knowledge of cultures and languages
  - participate in intercultural encounters
