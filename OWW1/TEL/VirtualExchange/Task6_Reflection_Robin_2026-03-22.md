The beginning of the interview started naturally. The AI asked me questions, I answered. At some point it stopped asking questions, so I asked if it had any more, and it said no, so we wrapped up the interview. However, I then realized the conversation had been WAY to short, so I restarted the recording and told the AI the conversation hadn't been in depth enough. From that point on, the conversation seemed to become more and more forced. I had to constantly prompt the AI for new questions, because it would just _stop talking_, and the questions also became more off-topic, so I had to steer it back more frequently. I would therefore say this task was easier than task 5 in the beginning, but harder in the end.

The AI was highly effective as cultural mediator. It really exposed the blind spots I had regarding Stefanos's culture, and things we could have talked about but did not. However, in comparison with a real journalist, the AI seemed less curious and also less structured. I had a hard time figuring out what it wanted from me. A real journalist is more steering, I think.

I am fairly certain that I represented my partners perspective properly, but am also very aware of the blind spots, that the AI has discovered. For example when it asked me about traditions and holidays. The question that made me think about my partners culture (and our interaction in general) the most, was the question about topics we had _not_ talked about. Having to explain my partners perspective really made me view things from his point of view. I think this is a good example of the 'decentering' we were supposed to do for this task. The moment that really clicked for me is when I explained how the night life is more important to them because of their busy day schedules. However, I do not think I stereotyped Stefanos in any ways. A small sidenote for honestly though: I had very little knowledge of Cyprus beforehand, so I don't think I know any stereotypes about Cypriots in the first place. Something that I do want to learn more about is the foods. What Stefanos has showed us looked very good, and I'd be interested in other local delicacies.

In comparison with task 5, I think this interview made me understand Stefanos's culture better, than the stakeholder perspectives did.

As I also said in my reflection for task 5, I did not notice any particilar cultural biases in the way the AI talked. What I did find annoying is that whenever you paused for a little too long because you were trying to formulate a sentence, the AI would start replying immediately. This led to me and the AI to talk through eachother on multiple occasions. At some point I think I screamed 'SHUT UP' because it would keep talking at the same time as me.
